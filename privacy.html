Privacy Notice

Last updated: November 28, 2026

What we collect

When you register your interest, we collect the details you choose to give us: your name, email, phone number, preferred suite types, ideal move-in date, household size and any notes you add.

When you visit this site, Google Analytics collects general usage information such as pages viewed, approximate location (city level), device and browser type, and how you arrived at the site. It uses cookies to do this. It does not receive the details you type into the form.

Why we collect it

We use your registration details only to contact you about leasing, pricing, availability and construction updates for 85 Wilson St West. We use analytics to understand how people find and use the site so we can improve it.

Who can see it

Your registration is stored with our website host (Netlify) and is seen only by the leasing team. Analytics data is processed by Google. Both providers may store data outside Canada. We do not sell or rent your information.

How long we keep it

We keep registration details until the building is leased up, or sooner if you ask us to delete them.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or to stop hearing from us, at any time by emailing info@85wilsonwest.ca. You can block analytics cookies in your browser settings or with Google's opt-out add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Questions

Email info@85wilsonwest.ca.
