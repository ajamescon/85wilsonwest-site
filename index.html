FAQ

Good questions.

When can I move in?

Move-in timelines are being finalized. Register your interest and you'll hear about dates and suite selection before anyone else.

What are the monthly rents?

Rates vary by suite and floor. Register your interest and we'll send current pricing for the layouts you choose.

Are appliances included?

Yes. Every suite comes with a full appliance package: fridge, stove, dishwasher, and an in-suite washer and dryer. Each suite also has its own hot water tank.

Is there parking?

Yes. Each suite includes 1 parking spot. Additional spots are available to rent monthly, subject to availability.

Is the building accessible?

The building has an elevator to every floor, and three suites are purpose-built barrier-free layouts with wide turning radii and an accessible bathroom: one-bedroom suites 202 and 204, and two-bedroom suite 302 on the top floor.

Which suites have outdoor space?

All nine two-bedroom suites in the 101/103/105 stacks (on every floor) have a private walk-out balcony off the living and dining area.
